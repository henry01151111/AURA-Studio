🌧️ AURA Studio v1.0.0: An Immersive Focus Space for Rainy Nights
Official Feature Breakdown & Product Guide
📌 Product Overview
AURA Studio is a web-based immersive focus space designed for deep learning, coding, writing, and meditation. It combines customizable rainy-scene visuals, a multi-track Web Audio mixer, guided mindful breathing, a native Document PiP (Picture-in-Picture) floating clock, and the classic Pomodoro technique to help users quickly enter a high-efficiency "Flow State" within an elegant, tranquil atmosphere.

Key Features & Technical Specs
User Benefits

🎨 Visuals & Dynamic Themes
11 themes (light & dark textures), simulated physical interactions of water droplets on glass, rendering of sliding raindrops and bokeh effects, and lightning animations.
Creates a personalized immersive environment suited to focus needs under varying lighting conditions (day/night).
🎧 Multi-track Focus Mixer
Real-time synthesis of rain sounds, binaural beats (Alpha/Beta/Theta waves), crackling fire, and wind chimes via Web Audio API; supports embedded YouTube background music.
Masks ambient noise; enhances logical reasoning and creative inspiration through brainwave entrainment.
⏱️ Pomodoro Timer & PiP Overlay
Supports switching between work/short break/long break, custom countdown durations, a circular progress ring, and Chrome's native Document PiP (floating window).
Seamlessly track focus progress and time management while multitasking across browser tabs.
🫁 Mindful Breathing Guide
Offers 4-4-4-4 (Box Breathing), 4-7-8 (Relaxation), and 5-5 (Equal Breathing) modes, accompanied by a pulsing visual ring and phase cues.
Quickly alleviates anxiety and stress; helps reset the brain and restore focus.
📝 Tasks & Draft Notes
Lightweight to-do list with real-time deletion and completion toggles; automatic draft note saving via LocalStorage. Quickly jot down spontaneous thoughts and to-do items without switching to external apps.
⚡ System Performance & Zen Mode
One-click low-power mode, full-screen toggle, auto-hiding UI after 5 seconds of inactivity (Zen immersive mode), and real-time FPS monitoring.
Conserve device battery, eliminate distractions, and create a clean, clutter-free visual experience.


🔍 In-depth Feature Analysis
1. Visuals & Canvas Interaction
Raindrop physics and droplet merging: Uses dynamic Canvas rendering; droplets flowing across the glass exhibit natural viscosity, pauses, and collision-merging effects.
Interactive glass smudging: With interaction enabled, mouse movement or touchscreen input displaces the raindrop particles.
11 Lock Screen Theme Cards:
Light Themes (Daytime): Morning Mist, Sunlit Shower, Cloudy Daylight.
Dark Themes (Nighttime): Night Library, Cozy Cafe, Deep Emerald, Cyber ​​Tokyo, Midnight Ocean, Sunset Glow, Nordic Aurora, Starlight Zen.
2. Multi-track Focus Audio Mixer
Web Audio API-based acoustic synthesis: Generates high-quality, immersive audio in real-time via code—no need to load large audio files.
Binaural Beats (Brainwave Frequency Selection):
Alpha Waves (10Hz): Ideal for deep focus and emotional calm.
Beta Waves (18Hz): Ideal for high-intensity tasks, logical analysis, and intensive coding.
Theta Waves (6Hz): Ideal for brainstorming, creative writing, and meditation. YouTube Background Streaming: Supports pasting YouTube video or 24-hour Lo-Fi livestream URLs; allows simultaneous listening to focus music while the audio mixer is active.
